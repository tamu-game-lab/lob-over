How to play

Plan

Schedule crews, commit orders and track spending. Field progress arrives in daily reports.

Site

Drive to a station, dismount and inspect. Speak observations to Plan and confirm orders at the crew.

Spec-keeper

Read the field manual. Supply permit codes and inspection numbers from its cards.

The loop

  1. The facilitator selects a scenario, opens the seats and starts the clock.
  2. Site observes the field; the spec-keeper reads the relevant card.
  3. Plan commits orders. Site reports whether they were carried out.
  4. The daily report records progress and spending. At the end, the result shows the planned and actual lines.

Autoplan runs the selected script's Plan commands. Site remains yours to operate.
