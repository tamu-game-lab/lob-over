# WO-L4 The site looks real

## Goal

Standing on the road in `Play.unity`, the headset player sees a construction site, not a drawing: textured ground with relief, a real trench with spoil, bedding and pipe, a gravel road, fences, cones and barriers, the trailer, the crews and machines, a sky with sun and clouds, shadows, and haze toward the horizon, inside the Quest 3S budget of `Docs/XR_RENDERING.md`. Every site material uses one shader, `LOBOver/SiteLit`, so WO-L5 can add the warp to all of them at once. Bryan's words on the first build: the assets are extremely poor, it does not feel realistic, a horizontal line like a drawing.

## Build

1. **Imports.** Add the four LOB-Game folders above to `Tools/import_lobgame_assets.ps1`; the orchestrator runs it before verification. Convert their Built-in materials to URP copies the way `SiteAssetBuilder` already does; every converted material ends on `LOBOver/SiteLit` (item 2) with its textures kept.
2. **`LOBOver/SiteLit`** (URP HLSL, `Art/SiteLit.shader`): opaque, lit through `UniversalFragmentPBR` with the main light and its shadows, SH ambient, fog; base map, normal map, smoothness and occlusion (a metallic-smoothness map where the source has one); tiling in world units for ground materials (triplanar for the ground mesh, so no stretching on slopes); a `_Splat` keyword variant for the ground that blends three tiled layers (dirt, gravel, dry grass) by vertex colour r, g, b; GPU instancing; the stereo macros of `XR_RENDERING.md`. One material per surface kind under `Art/Materials/`. The old `Soil`, `AccessRoad`, `Timber`, `StorageSteel`, `Repair_SiteCabin` copies move to this shader with the textures below.
3. **Textures.** If `Assets/` holds `TerrainLayer` assets or tileable textures whose names contain grass, dirt, soil, mud or gravel (a pack Bryan imported), use the best three for the ground layers and a dirt one for the trench. Otherwise generate them: an Editor generator writes tileable 1024² albedo, normal and smoothness textures to `Art/Generated/` for packed dirt (fBm with fine grain), gravel (Worley cells with varied stone tint), dry grass (streaked fBm, straw and olive), and a 256² soft dust sprite (radial falloff, brown, alpha) for the work particles. Normal maps from the height field; `sRGB` on albedo only; ASTC 6x6 on Android. LOB-Game's `groundTrenchDig` textures are the trench wall and bedding; the sand pile's albedo and occlusion are the spoil.
4. **Ground.** Replace the flat bank boxes with a built mesh, 1,200 m by 200 m, 2 m cells within 30 m of the trench and 10 m cells beyond (under 60,000 triangles), with gentle relief (fBm, 0.6 m amplitude, flat within 8 m of the trench and the road), vertex colours painting gravel on the road and its shoulders, dirt within 12 m of the trench, dry grass beyond, with noisy edges. A gravel road 4 m wide along the trench at `WorldUnits.RoadZ` with two shallow ruts. Beyond 100 m from the trench the ground continues as a simple plane to 3,000 m so the horizon never ends in a line against the sky. No colliders are needed on the site in Level 1 (the hub floor collider stays).
5. **Trench.** Per station cell, real geometry: a 1.2 m wide, 1.8 m deep cut with slightly irregular walls (noise-displaced), the trench wall texture; bedding gravel in the bottom when piped; LOB-Game's `PIPE.fbx` with its PBR textures as the laid pipe; a continuous spoil windrow on the far side from the road (noise-displaced mounds with the spoil texture, 1 m high, breaking every 30 m), the photoscanned sand pile as a hero pile at each crew's station; the slumped wall as a collapsed wedge with loose material on the floor; standing water as a dark reflective plane 0.3 m above the floor; seepage as a dark wet band on the lower wall; backfilled cells as a disturbed dirt strip at grade. Stakes: wooden lath 1.2 m, 5 cm wide, pink flagging, and a small station sign with the station text in 4 cm letters readable from the road.
6. **Props and surroundings.** Fences (`fence_long`) along the road's outer edge every 10 m for 60 m either side of each crew; cones and a barrier at the collapse and around each working machine; two jersey barriers at the gate; the trailer as a box with the site cabin material, a door and a window; a tree line (the lightest LOB-Game tree under 8,000 triangles, at most 30 instances, GPU instanced) 80 to 120 m beyond the far side with gaps; nothing within 20 m of the player that is a bare box.
7. **Sky and light.** A dome shader `LOBOver/SiteSky` (replaces `PlaySky`): if a cubemap skybox material exists under `Assets/` use its cubemap, else a procedural sky (zenith to horizon gradient, sun disc at the light's direction with a soft halo, two fBm cloud layers drifting slowly, a haze band at the horizon). The directional light: elevation 48°, warm white, soft shadows, one cascade, 2048, 60 m. Ambient trilight tuned to the sky. Linear fog 300 to 2,500 m in the horizon colour. Rain and the cloud bank keep working through `SiteSky`.
8. **Particles.** The work particles use the dust sprite on URP Particles/Unlit (alpha blended, soft, brown, small); no untextured white sprites anywhere.
9. **Settings** (values only): Mobile RP asset render scale 1.0, MSAA 4x, HDR off, no post-processing; `XRRuntimeSettings` foveation Low; static batching on the builder's static geometry; GPU instancing on repeated props.
10. **Scale sanity.** A worker is 1.8 m, the excavator cab roof about 3.2 m, the pipe 0.3 m outside diameter, the trench 1.2 m wide; the builder logs any imported model whose bounds are off by more than 25% and scales it.
11. **Tests.** EditMode: the builder runs twice and changes nothing the second time; every renderer under `[LOBOver] Site` uses `LOBOver/SiteLit`, `LOBOver/SiteSky` or URP Particles/Unlit; no material has a null main texture except the sky; the Editor stats at the pipe crew's station facing across the trench stay under 300k triangles and 150 draw calls (read through `UnityEditor.UnityStats`); the generated textures are tileable (the left and right edge columns match within 2%).
