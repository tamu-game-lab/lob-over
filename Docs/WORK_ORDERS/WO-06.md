# WO-06 — Host autoplay, slice fixtures, slice e2e

## Goal

`node Monitor/src/host/server.js Data/Scenarios/slice_texas.json --autoplan Monitor/test/scripts/slice_autoplan.json` runs the live host and applies the script's Plan commands at their sim times, so a single Site player (headset or `site.html`) experiences the whole chain without a Plan seat. The three slice scripts become test fixtures with pinned expectations, and the e2e covers the slice in under a minute.

## Build

1. `--autoplan <script.json>`: the host loads the script (same shape as headless scripts), ignores entries whose `seat` is `site` (a human has that seat), and applies each remaining entry when the paced sim reaches its `t` (before that tick), exactly as `runHeadless` does; acks and nacks are logged to stderr and shown as a line on the facilitator page ("autoplan: 8 commands, next at t 4.00"). `ctrl reset` restarts the script. The Plan page may still connect as a mirror.
2. Fixtures: `test/sim-slice.test.js` runs the three slice scripts headless and asserts the measured values with tolerances: `slice_play` finish 23.85 ± 0.1, idle 18.1 ± 0.2, collapse event at t 3.89 ± 0.02, conflict at t 4.1 ± 0.1, F1 caught 1; `slice_pull_play` finish 22.35 ± 0.1, idle 9.1 ± 0.2, protected card title "Wall movement at 5+20"; `slice_autoplan` finishes before the deadline with F1 missed. Determinism: two runs of `slice_play` byte-identical.
3. `e2e/slice.e2e.js`: the WO-03 e2e flow on the slice scenario with `ctrl fast`: Site rides to 5+20, the collapse card arrives on Site only, Plan commits the lend, the argument fires, Plan holds, the result appears with finish between 18 and 29. Under 60 s.
4. `Monitor/README.md`: add the `--autoplan` flag and the slice command line.
