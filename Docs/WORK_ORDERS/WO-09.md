# WO-09 — The inspector's verbs on the Quest: ride, dismount, gaze inspect, wrist clipboard, cards, voice, comfort

## Goal

With the headset on (Quest Link in the Editor first, the APK in WO-10), the Site player can do everything `site.html` can: ride the truck to a stake, dismount and mount, inspect by looking, flag, stop work, confirm orders, hear the foremen, read event cards and inspection text. Nothing else is added. Comfort first: no smooth locomotion, no vection, snap turns.

## Build

1. **Rig and truck.** The builder adds a `SiteTruck` (a cab from a 2.2 × 2 × 2.6 m box frame with an open front, a dashboard board 0.8 × 0.3 m at 1.1 m, a driver seat) parked on the haul road at the gate, and parents an `OVRCameraRig` (tracking origin floor, the seat at 1.15 m eye height) to the cab. Snap turn 30° with the right thumbstick, left thumbstick does nothing. `OVRManager` as in SampleScene; no passthrough.
2. **Ride.** The dashboard lists the next and previous stakes and the zone boards; pointing the right controller ray at a stake name and pulling the trigger (or thumbstick up/down to pick, A to go) sends `MoveTo`. The ride: fade to black over 0.3 s, play `dozer_engine_loop` for 1.5 s, reposition the cab at the stake (road side, facing +X), fade in over 0.3 s. The truck's position in the world follows the host's `truck.stationFt` when it changes, so a ride is never shown as motion. While `truck.moving` the dashboard reads "driving".
3. **Dismount and mount.** B button toggles; dismount moves the rig to a standing point 1.5 m from the trench edge at the stake, facing the trench; mount returns to the seat. Verbs that need the stake are enabled only when `truck.dismounted` is true (the host enforces it too; the client just greys the buttons).
4. **Inspect by looking.** A gaze ray from `CenterEyeAnchor` against colliders tagged `Inspectable` with an `InspectTarget` component (`wall`, `water`, `pipe`, `machine`, `protection`, `board`, `sky`, `ticket`, `crew`; WO-08 objects get the component, the sky target is a large collider dome, the ticket is on the gate); a thin reticle grows over 2.0 s of dwell, then `Inspect(target)` is sent once, with a 4 s cooldown per target. The reply text goes to the clipboard and is read with the Android `TextToSpeech` plugin when present (`AndroidJavaObject`; skipped in the Editor), nothing else.
5. **Wrist clipboard.** On the left controller anchor: a 0.22 × 0.16 m board with the last inspection text (TextMeshPro 12 pt equivalent), the day and speed line, and three physical buttons (3 × 2 × 1 cm, `SafetyOrange`): Flag, Stop work, Confirm. Pressing = right controller ray on the button + trigger; a button depresses 5 mm and clicks (`hydraulics_01` short). Flag sends `Flag(stake, "")` and plants a flag prefab at the stake. Stop work sends `StopWork(zone of the stake)`; the zone's board gets a red flag object (a fact, not a verdict). Confirm opens two buttons, Done / Not carried out, for the first order in `view.orders`; if none, the clipboard reads "no order landed here". Listen is automatic: within 10 m of `arguing` or `held` crews, play their lines from `voice` messages.
6. **Cards and voice.** `event` messages for seat site show a card on the dashboard and on the clipboard: title and facts, for 20 s or until a button press. `voice` messages: play `StreamingAssets/Voice/<stage>/<lineId>.wav` (your editor script `LOB Over/Build/Copy Voice` copies `Voice/` into StreamingAssets; load with `UnityWebRequestMultimedia` on Android) at the speaking crew's position when the line's speaker is a foreman, else at the dashboard; missing file = the text on the clipboard for 8 s.
7. **Comfort and failure modes.** Fixed cab frame always visible when mounted; a vignette during fades only; if `OnConnection` reports a disconnect, the dashboard reads "lost the office, reconnecting" and nothing moves; a `Settings` toggle on the dashboard for fade length (0.3 s / 0.6 s).
8. **Tests.** PlayMode `InputLayerTests` with a `FakeInput` seam (`IInspectorInput` interface implemented by `OvrInput` and `FakeInput`): dwell fires `Inspect` once after 2 s and not again within 4 s; a button press with the truck mounted is refused with the clipboard text; a `Flag` press sends `Flag` with the current stake; a ride sets the cab to the stake only after the state's `truck.stationFt` changes. No test may require a headset.
