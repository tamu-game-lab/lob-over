# WO-08 — The site in VR: scene built by script, world kept in step with `state`

## Goal

`Assets/LOBOver/Scenes/Site.unity`, created by an editor script, shows the slice's site at 1 ft = 0.3048 m from LOB-Game's imported assets, and a `SiteWorld` component spawns the per-station trench cells, the zone boards, the three machines and their crews from the first `state` message and updates them from every later one. No interaction yet beyond a desktop fly camera for checking; WO-09 adds the inspector's verbs and the XR rig.

## Build

1. **Builder** (`LOB Over/Build/Site Scene`): a new scene with URP settings as the project has them; directional light with a warm late-morning angle; a 1,200 m × 200 m ground plane with `Soil`; the haul road (`AccessRoad`, 8 m wide) parallel to the trench at 6 m offset, from −30 m to +950 m; the trench corridor placeholder (the cells are spawned at runtime); a gate at −20 m with two water barriers and a cone; the site trailer (a 6 × 3 × 3 m box with `Repair_SiteCabin`) at −15 m beside the road with a 2 × 1 m board on its wall; the job sign (a 1.2 × 0.8 m board on two timber posts) at the gate reading the `job.sign` values once known (the `state` carries `stage`; the builder leaves a `JobSign` component that `SiteWorld` fills from a `welcome`); a spoil pile instance every 100 ft on the far side of the trench; a `SiteWorld` object with the `SiteClient` from WO-07 and the `HostAddressPanel`; a `DesktopFlyCamera` (WASD + mouse, Editor only, disabled on Android).
2. **Stations** (`StationCell` prefab, your own): per 100 ft station a 30.48 m long cell: a trench 1.2 m wide, 1.8 m deep with vertical walls; state materials: `undug` = surface only (`Repair_Surface`), `open` = walls `Soil` and floor `Repair_OpenTrench`, `piped` = floor `Repair_PipeBed` plus three `ProjectPipeSegment` instances laid end to end, `backfilled` = `Repair_Backfill` level with the surface. `water: seep` = a dark wet band on the lower wall (a second material slot or a thin quad), `standing` = a flat water quad 0.2 m above the floor (your `Water.mat`, translucent). `wall: slumped` = a wedge mesh of soil leaning into the trench on the far wall and the water quad. `protection: box|shoring|slope` = a steel frame (two `StorageSteel` panels and three struts) / timber panels (`Timber`) / the walls tilted outward. A station stake at every 100 ft with the station text (TextMeshPro, 0.3 m, `5+20` form) facing the road. Zone boards at each zone's start: a 1 × 1.5 m board on a post with two text lines, "ZONE n" and the permit number or "no permit posted", and a third line with the inspection number when signed.
3. **Crews** (`CrewRig` prefab, your own): one machine prefab by `machinePlate` and crew id (plate "Standard" with crew `exc` → `Excavator_Standard`, etc.), placed with its working edge at `stationFt`, facing +X along the line; two `ProjectWorker` instances 2 m behind it on the road side; a plate (`TextMeshPro`, 0.25 m) on the machine reading the catalog label. Status → animation and posture: `working` = machine animator playing, workers Walk/Inspect alternating, `WorkParticles` on; `idle` = machine still, workers Idle, particles off; `held` = workers IdleAlternate facing the road; `stopped` = SignalHalt once then Idle; `arguing` = the two crews' foremen (first worker of each) walk to the midpoint and play ConflictTalk/TalkAlternate facing each other; `demobilized` = the rig is 60 m back toward the gate on the road with workers Shelter. Mood 0..3 = the workers' idle clip speed and a posture (0 Idle, 1 IdleAlternate, 2 Drink, 3 EquipmentCheck with SignalPoint every 10 s); no text, no colour. Movement between states eases over 0.5 s; a crew that moves more than 100 ft in one state update teleports.
4. **Sky and sound**: `clear` = default sky; `towersSouth` = a dark cumulus billboard bank 2 km to the south (−Z) and `bed_open_wind`; `rain` = a particle rain volume following the player, grey fog, `bed_rain_open`. Machine loops (`exc_work_loop`, `dozer_engine_loop`, `machine_idle_loop`) on each rig by status, attenuated 3D; `collapse_rumble_0x` once when a cell turns `slumped`; `conflict_murmur_loop` at an argument; `pipe_setdown` when a cell turns `piped`. Reuse `Assets/Resources/SiteAudioBank.asset` only if it is a plain data asset; otherwise reference clips directly.
5. **Trailer board**: a 2 × 1 m board texture drawn with a `RenderTexture` or a `LineRenderer` set: x = stations, y = days 1..deadline, the three crews' actual lines (from the `state`'s crews over time, accumulated client-side at each displayDay change) and vertical rules at the permit boundaries that are not yet posted. No planned lines, no money.
6. **SiteWorld**: subscribes to `SiteClient.OnState`; first state builds cells, boards, crews; later states diff and apply. Exposes `StationCell CellAt(int ft)`, `CrewRig Crew(string id)`, `Transform StakeAt(int ft)` for WO-09. Logs one line per changed cell or crew status at `Debug.Log` level Info (the orchestrator reads the console).
7. **Tests**: EditMode `SiteSceneBuilderTests` (the builder creates the scene with the expected object names and counts); PlayMode `SiteWorldTests` that feed `SiteWorld` three recorded `state` JSON fixtures (Day 1, Day 4 after the collapse at 5+20, Day 9 rain; take them from `Monitor/out/slice_play.json` snapshots' `site` view or ask the orchestrator for the file) and assert: cell 5+20 is `slumped` with standing water on Day 4, the pipe crew's status `stopped`, the backfill crew `arguing` on the Day-5 fixture, rain particles active on Day 9, board 2 reads "no permit posted" until the fixture with the permit. Plus a Play-mode screenshot method `LOB Over/Debug/Screenshot` that saves `Monitor/out/unity/<name>.png` (the orchestrator looks at it).
